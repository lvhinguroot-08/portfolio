## Technical Stack & Creative Direction

- **Zero-Dependency Architecture:** Clean, high-performance vanilla HTML5, CSS3, ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Transparent 3D Scene:** Three.js (r128) rendering an interactive cyber network sculpture with faceted metallic core, gimbal orbital rings, telemetry nodes, and orbiting sentinel indicators on a transparent WebGL canvas.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Deep obsidian and graphite (`#050608`, `#07080d`, `#10121b`)
  - Rich violet and royal purple (`#6d28d9`, `#7c3aed`, `#a78bfa`)
  - Refined electric blue and cyan (`#06b6d4`, `#38bdf8`)
  - Subtle silver highlights (`#f8fafc`, `#e2e8f0`, `#94a3b8`)
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans + Playfair Display + JetBrains Mono.
- **Accessibility & Performance:** Reduced motion fallback, keyboard focus visibility, semantic tags, ARIA attributes, WebGL power management (pauses rendering when tab is hidden or scrolled out of view).
- **SEO & Social:** Open Graph, Twitter Cards, Schema.org Person JSON-LD, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, canonical link.
